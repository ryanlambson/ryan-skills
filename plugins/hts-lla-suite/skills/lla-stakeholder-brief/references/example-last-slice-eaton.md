# Worked example — Last Slice Eaton (Tavern Restricted Licence, WA)

Model output. Conventions-compliant. This is the standard to match.

Source PIA: Last Slice Eaton Pty Ltd (ACN 673 298 498), Tavern Restricted Licence,
Shop T105, Eaton Fair Shopping Centre, 2 Recreation Drive, Eaton WA 6232. Drafted
02-07-2026.

---

**Subject:** Pre-Lodgement Advice – Application for the Conditional Grant of a Tavern
Restricted Licence – Last Slice Eaton, Eaton Fair Shopping Centre

Dear [Recipient],

We write to inform you of an application to be lodged with the Licensing Authority by
Last Slice Eaton Pty Ltd (ACN 673 298 498) for the conditional grant of a Tavern
Restricted Licence pursuant to Section 41 of the Liquor Control Act 1988. The proposed
premises, Last Slice Eaton, is located at Shop T105, Eaton Fair Shopping Centre,
2 Recreation Drive, Eaton WA 6232.

The premises forms part of the Eaton Fair Shopping Centre, the centrepiece of the Eaton
Town Centre, which is designated as the District Centre of the Shire of Dardanup under
Local Planning Scheme No. 9. The Applicant is directed by Mr Jeremy Doble and Mr Casey
Teede, both Approved Managers, whose Last Slice venues in Bunbury, Dalyellup and
Midland include the AHA Awards for Excellence recipient Last Slice Bunbury (Best
Sporting Entertainment Venue 2022; Redeveloped Venue and Sporting Entertainment Venue
2024).

The Applicant aims to:

- Operate a quality, family-friendly dining and entertainment venue featuring Last
  Slice's signature New York-style pizza, a dedicated kids' corner, sports lounge,
  electronic darts, alfresco dining and function hosting, serving Eaton consumers and
  visitors to the locality;
- Provide a responsible service environment anchored in a substantial food offering,
  consistent with the standards applied across the existing Last Slice venues; and
- Support the Shire of Dardanup's strategic vision to consolidate the Eaton Town
  Centre as the commercial and cultural centre of the Shire by broadening the
  hospitality offering available within the District Centre.

Subject to approval in the public interest, the following Trading Conditions will be
implemented at all times:

1. The licensee is authorised to sell and supply liquor in accordance with the
   provisions of Section 41 of the Act as it relates to a Tavern Restricted Licence.
2. The sale of packaged liquor for consumption off the licensed premises is prohibited.
3. The licensee is to have and maintain a CCTV system at the entry and exit points of
   the premises in accordance with the Director of Liquor Licensing's policies.
4. Seating will be available for up to 50% of patrons at all times, with the exception
   of pre-booked functions or events.
5. A range of food will be available at all times until one hour before close.
6. Drink options that contain low alcohol liquor content, as well as a range of
   non-alcoholic drink options, must be available at all times.
7. Liquor is not to be promoted or sold in a manner that would encourage its rapid
   consumption.
8. Dress Standards – Outlaw Motorcycle Gang members are not permitted on the premises.

The venue will provide a safe, professionally managed licensed meeting place serving
the requirements of consumers within the Eaton locality, supported by a comprehensive
Harm Minimisation Plan, mandatory RSA training and an Approved Manager on the premises
at all times.

We appreciate any feedback you may have regarding this Tavern Restricted Licence
application.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

## Why this is the model

- Conditions verbatim from the PIA, renumbered; packaged liquor line matches the
  tavern restricted class (fully prohibited, no lodger exception).
- CCTV stated as entry and exit points only.
- No banned terms, no em-dashes in paragraphs (subject-line separators permitted).
- No claim of recipient support; closes with a feedback invitation only.
