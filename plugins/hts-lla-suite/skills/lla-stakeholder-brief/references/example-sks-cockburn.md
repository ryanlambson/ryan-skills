# Worked example — SKS Cockburn Central (Hotel Restricted Licence, WA)

STRUCTURE REFERENCE ONLY. This example predates lla-writing-conventions: it contains
"food-focused" and other wording that would now fail the conventions check. Copy the
structure, never the phrasing.

---

We write to inform you of an application lodged with the Licensing Authority by SKS
Cockburn Central Hotel Pty Ltd (ACN 604 689 125) for a Hotel Licence pursuant to
Section 41 of the Liquor Control Act 1988. The proposed hotel premises, Holiday Inn
Express Cockburn Central, is located at Lot 7, 28 Signal Terrace, Cockburn Central,
WA 6164.

The premises forms part of a $100 million, 13-level mixed-use development currently
under construction within the Cockburn Central Activity Centre. The development
comprises 170 hotel rooms (3-star classification), 109 build-to-rent residential
apartments, and 210 square metres of retail space. The hotel will operate under the
Holiday Inn Express brand, part of the InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG).

The Applicant aims to:

- Operate a quality hotel with a small restaurant, bar and meeting facilities serving
  up to 85 hotel guests and the general public at any one time;
- Provide a food-focused, responsible service environment consistent with
  international hotel brand standards; and
- Support the City of Cockburn's vision for Cockburn Central as the "capital of
  Perth's South Metro Region" by providing quality accommodation and hospitality
  services for business and leisure travellers.

Subject to approval in the public interest, the following Trading Conditions will be
implemented at all times:

1. The licensee is authorised to sell and supply liquor in accordance with the
   provisions of Section 41 of the Act as it relates to a Hotel Restricted Licence.
2. Packaged liquor, for consumption off the premises, may only be sold to lodgers.
3. The licensee is to have and maintain a CCTV system in accordance with the Director
   of Liquor Licensing's policies.
4. Seating and associated table arrangements must be available except for when there
   is a pre-booked function or event.
5. Food will be available at all times until one hour before close.
6. Drink options that contain low alcohol liquor content, as well as a range of
   non-alcoholic drink options, must be available at all times.
7. Dress Standards – Outlaw Motorcycle Gangs not permitted

The hotel will provide essential accommodation and hospitality services to support
business travellers, visitors to the activity centre, and the growing residential and
employment population in the locality.

We appreciate any feedback you may have regarding this Hotel Restricted application.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

---

## Structural takeaways

- Opening sentence formula: entity + ACN + licence class + s.41 + premises + address.
- Second paragraph: development context and operator pedigree.
- Three aims: operation, responsible service, strategic alignment (quote the LGA's
  own vision).
- Conditions block: verbatim from the application, renumbered.
- Close: one value sentence, feedback invitation, thanks.
- Note the hotel restricted packaged liquor line (lodgers only) vs the tavern
  restricted prohibition — this is the licence-class-keyed sentence.
