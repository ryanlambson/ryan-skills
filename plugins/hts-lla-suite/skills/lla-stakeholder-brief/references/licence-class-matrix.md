# Licence-class matrix — s.41 Liquor Control Act 1988 (WA)

Guides the packaged liquor sentence, capacity note, and emphasis of each brief.
**The PIA's own trading conditions block is always the source of truth. If the PIA
conflicts with this matrix, stop and flag the conflict (Phase A/Troubleshooting); do
not silently follow either.**

| Licence class | Packaged liquor sentence | Capacity / other notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tavern | As conditioned in the PIA (packaged sales may be authorised) | Trading hours per s.98(1) |
| Tavern Restricted | "The sale of packaged liquor for consumption off the licensed premises is prohibited." | Trading hours per s.98(1); maximum numbers per Maximum Accommodation Certificate unless the PIA states a lesser approved number |
| Hotel | As conditioned in the PIA (packaged sales may be authorised) | Accommodation obligations apply |
| Hotel Restricted | "Packaged liquor, for consumption off the premises, may only be sold to lodgers." | Accommodation obligations apply |
| Small Bar | "The sale of packaged liquor is prohibited." | Capacity 150 persons (patrons plus staff) per the 2025 amendments — never 120; see hts-small-bar-standards |

## Standard condition set (carry from the PIA, typical order)

1. s.41 authorisation line for the class
2. Packaged liquor line (per class, above)
3. CCTV — entry and exit points of the premises, per the Director's policies
4. Seating availability (percentage or arrangement per the PIA)
5. Food availability until one hour before close
6. Low-alcohol and non-alcoholic range available at all times
7. No promotion encouraging rapid consumption (where the PIA includes it)
8. Dress standards — OMCG exclusion

## Jurisdiction note

NSW matters use the Liquor Act 2007 (NSW) equivalents; do not transpose WA section
numbers. Route NSW drafts through lla-citation-checker before delivery.
