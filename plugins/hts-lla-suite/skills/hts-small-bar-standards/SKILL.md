# HTS Small Bar Drafting Standards (WA)

This skill encodes HTS house standards for any document describing a Western Australian
small bar. It is a **drafting-convention and supporting-evidence layer** — it never overrides
legislation, regulation, Director's Policy or case law. Where a rule states a regulatory fact,
the primary source is cited and must be re-verified at lodgement through the HTS pre-delivery
gate (Stage 1 lla-citation-checker, Stage 2 lla-compliance-checker).

Apply §§2–8 as drafting defaults; treat §1 as a hard regulatory check.

The deeper reference module is `references/KNOWLEDGE_SmallBar_Drafting.md`; the stale-fact
detail is `references/stale-fact-small-bar-capacity.md`. Load them when drafting or auditing.

---

## 1. Regulatory facts (HARD CHECK — verify at lodgement)

- **Small bar maximum capacity = 150 persons (patrons PLUS staff) at any one time.**
  Source: *Liquor Control Act 1988* (WA), ss.41A/41B, as amended by the 2025 amendment
  package (increase from 120 to 150). **Never state "120". Never frame 150 as patrons-only —
  it is patrons plus staff.** Existing small bars may apply via Form 11 (fee waived) to lift
  120→150, subject to a safe-capacity assessment.
- **Stale-citation guard:** WARN on any "120" appearing in a small bar capacity context in any
  draft, and suggest the 150 (patrons + staff) replacement. This mirrors the
  lla-citation-checker stale-citations registry entry.
- A small bar authorises sale of liquor **without a requirement that it be ancillary to a
  meal** — the defining distinction from a Restaurant licence, which drives §§2–3 below.

## 2. Terminology

- Use **"patrons"**, not "persons", for capacity (e.g. "100 patrons").
- **Drop "dining"** in small bar contexts: **"alfresco area"** not "alfresco dining area";
  **"patron areas"** not "dining areas". "Dining" implies meal-tied service and mislabels a
  small bar.
- Council pathway: write **"DA or Section 40 Certificate"** — a change of use to a small bar
  may be processed either way; do not presume one.

## 3. Manner of trade — state the small-bar-defining features explicitly

Always include, where true:

- Patrons may **stand and mingle** and **need not be seated at all times** (key distinction
  from a restaurant). Seating provided throughout, but not mandatory.
- Patrons may **purchase liquor from the bar and carry it to a table or elsewhere** within the
  licensed area (not table-service-only).
- Capacity for **private cocktail bookings** where patrons socialise.
- Entertainment is **background in nature** — typically a **solo or duo acoustic artist**, not
  amplified or headline entertainment.

## 4. Right-size impact sections — do not claim mitigation for impacts that do not arise

- For a **background-music-only** venue, do not recite heavy noise-engineering (e.g.
  mechanical ventilation specifically for entertainment, "comprehensive noise measures") and
  do not list "noise management" among precinct emphases where it overstates the issue.
- Where operations are unchanged, **soften servicing content** (waste, noise) to describe
  continuity rather than asserting new facilities or controls that are not evidenced.

## 5. Anchor every "no change" argument to the causal hook

State the causation, do not merely assert continuity:

- "**No increase in patron numbers → no change to the waste / noise management plans.**"
- "As the proposal involves no change to the patron number, there is no change to the
  premises' waste servicing requirements."

## 6. Food & beverage description

Use **concrete, real detail** (e.g. "contemporary, modern Australian food, including share
plates and lighter, healthier options") rather than generic "quality meals" — supports
the harm minimisation and amenity case and reads as a genuine hospitality offer.

## 7. Normalise the use

Note small bars are an **established, contemporary format adopted across many entertainment and
dining precincts across WA** — reduces perceived novelty/risk and supports the public-interest
case.

## 8. Document mechanics

- **Figure captions:** use Word SEQ caption fields (auto-renumbering), not hard-coded
  "Figure 1:" numbers. Check for duplicate sequence numbers before lodgement.
- **Attachment list must match what is actually lodged** — never carry template attachments
  (e.g. Certificates of Title, Sample Menu) that are not part of the bundle.
