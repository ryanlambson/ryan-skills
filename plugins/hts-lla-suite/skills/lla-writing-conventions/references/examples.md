# Before / After Examples

Worked rewrites for each writing convention. Use these as the model when correcting drafts.

## "sits" → located / situated

- Before: The premises sits within the Eaton Town Centre.
- After: The premises is located within the Eaton Town Centre.

- Before: The venue sits adjacent to the main retail strip.
- After: The venue is situated adjacent to the main retail strip.

## "food-led" → hospitality

- Before: The applicant proposes a food-led venue with ancillary liquor service.
- After: The applicant proposes a hospitality venue with ancillary liquor service.

- Before: This is a food led offering, not a tavern.
- After: This is a hospitality offering, not a tavern.

## "need" → requirement

- Before: The applicant needs to demonstrate that the grant is in the public interest.
- After: The applicant is required to demonstrate that the grant is in the public interest.

- Before: There is a clear need for this service in the locality.
- After: There is a clear requirement for this service in the locality.

## "community" → consumer

- Before: The proposal meets the needs of the local community.
- After: The proposal meets the requirements of the local consumer.

- Before: Surrounding communities will benefit from the amenity.
- After: Surrounding consumers will benefit from the amenity.

## CCTV wording

- Before: CCTV will monitor all internal and external areas of the premises.
- After: CCTV is located at the entry and exit points of the premises.

- Before: The premises will be fully covered by CCTV throughout.
- After: CCTV is located at the entry and exit points of the premises.

## Em-dashes inside paragraphs

- Before: The premises is located in the town centre — a high-amenity area — and serves daytime patrons.
- After: The premises is located in the town centre (a high-amenity area) and serves daytime patrons.

- Before: The applicant holds extensive experience — over twenty years in licensed hospitality.
- After: The applicant holds extensive experience: over twenty years in licensed hospitality.

- Before: The grant is in the public interest — the evidence supports this conclusion.
- After: The grant is in the public interest. The evidence supports this conclusion.

## Protected quotes — do NOT substitute

- Leave intact: Section 38(2) of the Liquor Control Act 1988 refers to "the requirements of the community" — because this is verbatim legislation, "community" stays.
