# Pre-lodgement brief — canonical structure

Every slot is filled from the completed PIA. If a slot has no PIA source, stop
(Phase A stop rule). Delete the guidance comments before presenting.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Rules

- Never state or imply endorsement by any recipient.
- No em-dashes inside paragraphs; the subject-line en-dash separators are permitted.
- Conditions never re-typed from this template; always verbatim from the PIA.
